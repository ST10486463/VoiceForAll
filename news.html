VICTIMS STORIES:

Real stories that remind us why the fight against GBV must continue.

Uyinene “Nene” Mrwetyana

Uyinene "Nene" Mrwetyana (20 April 2000 – 24 August 2019) was a South African student at the University of Cape Town. On August 24, 2019, she was raped and murdered in the suburb of Claremont, Cape Town. Her murder highlighted the broader national problem of gender based violence and femicide in South Africa, and is credited with "shifting the South African collective consciousness" and "igniting a movement".

Anene Booysen

Anene Booysen (October 30, 1995 – February 2, 2013) was a 17-year-old girl who was found by a security guard the morning after she had been gang-raped and disemboweled at a construction site in Bredasdorp, in the Western Cape, South Africa on February 2, 2013; she was still alive, but died later in the day. Anene Booysen was found by a security guard lying a short distance from her house after spending time at a bar on the evening of February 2, 2013. She died from her injuries in hospital six hours later. She had managed to allegedly identify her attackers before her death. It is unknown why the police chose to ignore all but one.

Mbali Hill & Nompumelelo Gumede

Mbali Hill, 23, and Nompumelelo Gumede, 21, were brutally murdered in Empumelelweni, eMalahleni. It is alleged that Nompumelelo had been in an argument with her boyfriend, Mpumelelo Ignitus Hill, when Mbali, who was the boyfriend's sister, tried to intervene. Mpumelelo then allegedly doused both women in petrol, set them alight, and locked the room. Both women were rushed to the hospital, but Mbali succumbed to her severe injuries on 1 August 2025, followed two days later by Nompumelelo on 2 August 2025. The suspect was arrested and charged with murder. He has since withdrawn his bail application and will remain in custody until his next court appearance in October 2025.

Linda Makhubo

Linda Makhubo’s lifeless body was found in Meadowlands Zone 6, Soweto, on 9 June 2025. He was last seen on 4 June after allegedly taking an e-hailing service. His family identified his lifeless body on 9 June in a mortuary. Linda was a teacher at Tetelo Secondary School in Protea North and was deeply loved by his students.

Tyrelle Jonker

Tyrelle Jonker, 17, was shot to death in Seawinds in Lavender Hill, Cape Town, on 6 March 2025. It’s alleged that the suspect knocked on Tyrelle’s front door and then opened fire right outside his home, where he succumbed to his injuries. When his family returned home, they found Tyrelle’s lifeless body. The unknown suspect fled the scene. Tyrelle was a Grade 11 learner at Steenberg High School and a well-known soccer player in his community. He leaves behind his 11-month-old baby. No arrests have been made.

Three Siblings — Poisoned

Moeketsi Mokhatla, 9, Tshepiso Tsotetsi, 9, and Rethabile Mokhatla, 11, were allegedly poisoned and murdered in Evaton, Gauteng, on 12 August 2024. It is alleged that the three siblings were walking to school when they encountered their stepfather, who allegedly gave them cookies. A few hours later, their mother received a call from the school informing her that all of her children had fallen unconscious. When the mother arrived, they had all tragically passed away. A postmortem has been conducted, which confirmed that the children were poisoned. No one has been arrested to this day.

Jacobus Benjamin

Jacobus William Benjamin, 48, was allegedly stabbed to death by his girlfriend in his home in Retreat, Cape Town, on 9 August 2024. It is alleged that Jacobus’s lifeless body was found lying outside the toilet in his home. He was declared deceased at the scene by medical personnel. His partner and the mother of his daughter was arrested shortly afterwards in connection with the murder. However, she was released on bail two weeks later. It is alleged that Jacobus wanted to end the relationship, but his girlfriend refused to accept his decision.

Jayden-Lee Meek

Jayden-Lee Meek, 11, body was found on the staircase of the complex where his family lives in Fleurhof, West Rand of Johannesburg, on 14 May 2025. It is alleged that he was found undressed and with multiple bruises on his body. His family had reported him missing the day before after his transport dropped him off outside the complex. In the early hours of the morning, the family was notified that Jayden-Lee’s body had been discovered in a corridor within the complex. The family rushed him to the hospital, hoping he was still alive, but he was declared deceased upon arrival. In July 2025, weeks after Jayden-Lee was brutally murdered, his mother was arrested in connection with her son's murder. On 29 July, she was denied bail.

Ntokozo Masilela Ngwenya

Ntokozo Masilela Ngwenya, 21, was murdered during an armed robbery at a filling station in Daveyton, Ekurhuleni, on 5 May 2025. It is alleged that the driver of the e-hailing vehicle Ntokozo was in - whose identity remains unknown - attempted to flee the scene when a group of armed robbers opened fire, fatally shooting both of them with an AK-47. The driver was declared dead at the scene, and Ntokozo was rushed to hospital, where she later succumbed to her injuries. No arrests have been made at this time.

Sinenhlanhla Mathonsi

Sinenhlanhla Mathonsi, 22, was allegedly stabbed to death by her boyfriend at a ‘Pick n Pay’ shop at Protea Glen Mall in Soweto on 4 May 2025. It is alleged that Sinenhlanhla, who worked at the Pick n Pay shop, was still on duty when her boyfriend arrived at the store, brutally attacking and repeatedly stabbing her. Sinenhlanhla sustained several stab wounds to her upper body and was certified dead at the scene. The boyfriend, 26, was arrested. The family stated that they were not aware of any previous domestic violence incidents between the couple.
