Our Services

We offer a range of support services aimed at helping survivors of Gender-Based Violence (GBV) rebuild, recover, and regain their strength.

Counseling & Emotional Support

Confidential one-on-one or group healing sessions with trained counselors.

24/7 Helpline

Call, WhatsApp, or email anytime for urgent support and guidance.

Legal support

Legal aid and help with reporting GBV, protection orders, and rights awareness.

Shelters & Safe Spaces

Temporary housing for victims needing protection and safety.

Medical Assistance

Support with accessing hospitals, clinics, and medical examinations.

Training & Empowerment

Skills development, awareness campaigns, and GBV prevention programs.
