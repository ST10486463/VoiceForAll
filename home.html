Understanding Gender-Based Violence

What is GBV?
GBV includes physical, emotional, sexual, and psychological harm directed at a person based on their gender.
Signs of Abuse
Control, manipulation, isolation, threats, intimidation, financial control, or physical harm.
How You Can Help
Support victims, provide resources, speak out, donate, volunteer, or report incidents.
