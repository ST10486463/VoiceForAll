About VoiceForAll

VoiceForAll is an organization that is there to support both genders in Gender-Based violence, it is a dedicated organization that creates awarness to ensure that every viticm is heard,supported and empowerd. Our organization foucuses on protecting surviours, providing them with a safe shelters and supporting them through their healing jounery because we believe that Every Voice Matters, Every Life Counts. Our work goes beyond by empowering individuals with knowledge and assistance, we are there to build a society where everyone can live free from fear, violence and discrimination.

Brief History:

The VoiceForAll Foundation was founded in 2018 by Oratile Matlhabane who grew up in realization that Gender-Based-Violence affects both genders. Oratile is passionate advocate for equality and social justice who witnessed the deep impact Gender-Based-Violence has on both genders. She grew up in an environment where Gender-Based-Violence was widespread but rarely spoke about. While growing up she observed how survivors were often silenced, with many survivors not receiving support and care which the whole situations motivated her to start an organization that would not only support survivors but would also embrace all and break the silence. After 2 years of volunteering to be part of youth GBV support groups, she began to work with small teams of community groups, social workers and legal professions to talk about the new idea she has on staring an organization then she started getting fundings from community groups and local businesses to start her organization called VoiceForAll Foundation.

Mission:

The mission for VoiceForAll Foundation it is there as an organization to support and take care of both gender’s male and female from Gender-Based-Violence by providing them with counseling, shelter, legal assistance and hosting campaigns that would raise awareness about Gender-Based-Violence while working to embrace all and break the silence.

Vision:

VoiceForAll visions a society where all survivors of Gender-Based-Violence are supported and empowered and where every voice and all stories are heard and voices replace silence and equality replaces fear.

Goals & Objectives:

  • Advocating for justice and equality.
  • Breaking the silence about Gender-Based Violence.
  • Providing safe spaces and support for victims.
  • Raise more awareness and do campaigns to empower and give people knowledge about GBV.
  • Coming up with strategies that might end GBV.
  • Provide access services such as shelters, legal assistance and counselling.
  • Build partnerships
